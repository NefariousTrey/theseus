Theseus

Blast radius that leaves only what's load-bearing.

Vultr Cloud Compute Vultr Serverless Inference bwrap lockbox

Theseus copies your project, cuts one part at a time from the copy, runs your tests inside a locked sandbox, and records what broke. The tests decide every verdict. An AI agent on Vultr Serverless Inference picks the cut order and explains each break in plain words. Your original is never written. Approve only exports a new, trimmed copy and re-runs the tests on it.

Run a scan

When on, part names and paths, short code snippets of broken parts (up to 40 lines each) and error lines are sent to Vultr Serverless Inference. Secret-looking strings are scrubbed first. Verdicts always come from the tests.

Containment demo

A hostile dependency tries to phone home, write to your home folder, read your API key, fork-bomb and eat memory. Watch it fail.
